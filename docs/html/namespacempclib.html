template<typename T, typename = std::enable_if_t<std::is_arithmetic<T>::value>>
T mpclib::sinc ( T x)
inlinestatic

Numerically stable normalized sinc function.

Computes \( \mathrm{sinc}(x) = \sin(x)/x \).
For \( |x| < 10^{-3} \) a 4th‑order Taylor series approximation is returned to avoid catastrophic cancellation:

\[ \mathrm{sinc}(x) \approx 1 - \frac{x^{2}}{6}. \]
