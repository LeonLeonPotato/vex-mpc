Numerically stable normalized sinc function. 

Computes $ \mathrm{sinc}(x) = \sin(x)/x $. ~\newline
 For $ |x| < 10^{-3} $ a 4th‑order Taylor series approximation is returned to avoid catastrophic cancellation:\+   \[    \mathrm{sinc}(x) \approx 1 - \frac{x^{2}}{6}.
\]
